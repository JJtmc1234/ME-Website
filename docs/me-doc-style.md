# ME Docs style

How ME documents are formatted in Google Docs. Repository documentation follows
Markdown conventions instead, but the two should agree on hierarchy and tone.

## Type

| Level | Size |
| --- | --- |
| Title | 24 pt |
| Heading 1 | 18 pt |
| Heading 2 | 15 pt |
| Heading 3 | 13 pt |
| Body | 12 pt |

Body text is 12 pt JetBrains Mono. This replaced an earlier 22 pt body, which
was large enough that a paragraph filled a screen and structure became hard to
see. Headings now carry the hierarchy, rather than everything being big.

Pageless layout where the document allows it. Most ME documents are read on a
screen and never printed, and pagination only adds breaks that mean nothing.

## Tone

The same as the repositories: short plain sentences, no dashes or semicolons,
and no filler. Say what was decided and why. A document that states a plan
should say what has actually been done and what has not, in words that cannot be
misread as a claim that something exists.

## Applying it

These settings are applied by hand in Google Docs, through the document's
styles. Nothing in this repository writes to Drive, and nothing should: the
tooling here has no business editing company documents.
